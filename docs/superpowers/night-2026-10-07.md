## Known limits left in place

- Stats: one current UTC offset is applied to all days. After a DST change, rows within an hour of midnight move by a day, and the whole cache is rebuilt once. Moscow time has no DST.
- Stats: an all-zero Codex record counts as a request.
- MSI: it installs for all users and needs administrator rights. Its uninstaller has none of the NSIS hooks: it leaves `%LOCALAPPDATA%\Mayak\{bin,inbox,mayak.log}` behind and does not remind you about the Codex integration. Do not install both the MSI and the setup.exe.
