Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026
In short: Mayak has no account, no analytics, no ads and no tracking. The developer receives none of your data. Everything stays on your computer, except what you choose to send to the services you connect yourself.

1. Who is responsible

Mayak is published by mahach666, an individual developer (“we”). Contact: open an issue at github.com/mahach666/mayak.

2. Data we collect

None. Mayak sends no data to us or to any server we operate. There is no telemetry, no crash-reporting service and no third-party SDK.

4. Services you connect yourself

Mayak talks only to the services you configure, directly from your computer, with your own keys:

  • GitHub (github.com, api.github.com) and GitLab (gitlab.com or the address you saved) — your repositories, pull requests or merge requests, issues and CI. GitHub connects through a read-only GitHub App that sees only the repositories you install it on.
  • Stripe, Vercel, n8n, Resend, Notion, Cal.com — read-only data from your own accounts, to show it on tiles.
  • OpenRouter, DeepSeek, Moonshot — your balance, when you enter a key.

Those services process your data under their own privacy policies. We have no access to these requests or their responses.

5. Your agents

Claude Code, Codex and Antigravity send session events to Mayak through a local connection on your computer (a named pipe); nothing leaves it. Mayak reads their session files to list the sessions and their limits. If an agent's hook cannot reach Mayak, it carries on without it.

The Agents tab runs your own agent CLIs (Claude Code, Codex, Gemini CLI, Cursor Agent) in a folder you choose, with the login you already have in them. Mayak makes no network request for it and stores no key for it; what the CLI sends to its provider is covered by that provider's terms and privacy policy.

The optional MCP server (off by default) listens on 127.0.0.1 only and answers only requests that carry its token. The optional Agent Orchestrator integration reads that tool's local daemon, read-only.

6. Children

Mayak is a tool for programmers and collects no data from anyone.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the date above changes with it. The history is in the repository.
